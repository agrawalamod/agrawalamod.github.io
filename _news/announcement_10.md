title: "Connectivity Standards Alliance (CSA) — Matter Member Meeting, Barcelona (Q4 2025)"
excerpt: "Represented Amazon at the CSA Matter Member Meeting in Barcelona, working with other industry leaders to define interoperability standards for wireless sensing, proximity ranging, and RF data exchange. This included efforts to standardize Wi-Fi CSI sensing, Ambient audio sensing, Bluetooth channel sounding, Ultra-wideband (UWB) ranging, and Wi-Fi Round Trip Time (RTT). Amazon also showcased a proximity detection demo using BLE RSSI, presenting a technique that lowers the barrier to entry for hardware manufacturers."
