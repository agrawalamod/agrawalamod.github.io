title: "Project HAMS nominated for Prime Minister's Award (2020) & featured by the Govt. of India"
excerpt: 'The HAMS project at Microsoft Research India on automated driver license testing was shortlisted for the Prime Minister''s Award in 2020 and is featured in the Government of India''s coffee table book on "Innovation." In collaboration with the Uttarakhand Transport Department and the Institute of Driver Training and Research, HAMS was first deployed in Dehradun and has since been rolled out across multiple states, with more than 58,000 automated tests conducted to date.'
